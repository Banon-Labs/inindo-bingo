recruitable ally town (no recruit)

Why is nobody recruitable at the start? Each character secretly carries an AI order ("busy" vs "standing idle") and you can only recruit someone while they're idle. Everyone starts the game with a busy order. Orders are re-rolled by a scheduling pass that runs at story beats — not every dawn — so a character flips to recruitable only after a story event re-rolls their order to idle. Don't take our word for it: every character sheet below has a "Validate in your emulator" list — put those addresses in your emulator's RAM watch, warp to the town, and check the values yourself. A simulator lever that predicts these re-rolls ahead of time is planned.
